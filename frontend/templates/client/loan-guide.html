RinSathi

Loan Criteria Guide

Follow this workflow before applying. RinSathi checks verified identity, income strength, collateral coverage, credit history, regulatory flags, and the final qualification score before a loan officer makes the final decision.

Key approval lines
Recommend 65+ / 100
Manual review 40 to 64
Reject range Below 40
Land LTV cap 75%
Vehicle LTV cap 85%
1

Verify Identity

NIN is checked against DoNIDCR. Active, valid identity is required before scoring.

2

Confirm Assets

Land is verified through NeLIS, or vehicle value is checked for vehicle loans.

3

Prove Income

At least one income source is needed from wallet, remittance, or cooperative records.

4

Check Risk

The model scores repayment probability using income, assets, sector, and stability.

5

Officer Decision

AI recommends, rejects, or refers. A loan officer remains responsible for the final outcome.

Identity and KYC
  • Enter a valid National Identity Number exactly as issued.
  • The NIN must exist in DoNIDCR and belong to an active citizen.
  • Applications with failed identity verification are sent to manual review under KYC_INCOMPLETE.
Loan Request
  • Loan amount must be greater than NPR 0.
  • You cannot submit another loan while one application is pending or referred.
  • Select a sector such as agriculture, retail, services, manufacturing, livestock, construction, transport, education, healthcare, or other.
Income Proof
  • Provide at least one income source with a positive monthly amount.
  • Accepted sources are Digital Banking, remittance, and cooperative savings.
  • Income confidence below 25% is flagged as INCOME_UNVERIFIABLE.
  • Zero income with a loan above NPR 10,000 is rejected as NO_INCOME_SIGNALS.
Collateral Coverage
  • For microfinance, verified land value is used as collateral.
  • Requested loan should not exceed 75% of verified land value.
  • If no verified collateral exists, the application is flagged as NO_VERIFIED_COLLATERAL.
  • Vehicle loans should not exceed 85% of verified vehicle value.
Credit and Compliance
  • CIB blacklist status is a hard rejection signal.
  • 90+ days past due history triggers manual review as SEVERE_DELINQUENCY_HISTORY.
  • Agriculture or livestock loans above NPR 500,000 are flagged for sector exposure.
  • Monthly income above NPR 1,000,000 is flagged for AML review.
Qualification Score
  • AI repayment probability contributes 35% of the final score.
  • Asset coverage contributes 20%.
  • Income reliability contributes 15%.
  • Credit history contributes 15%, and clean compliance contributes 15%.

Recommended

Score is 65 or higher, no compliance flags, and the application moves to officer confirmation.

Manual Review

Score is in the middle range, identity or credit history needs review, or a non-hard compliance issue exists.

Rejected

Hard flags such as blacklist, major collateral breach, no income signals, AML flag, or score below 40 can reject the application.
